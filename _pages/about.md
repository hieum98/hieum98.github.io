I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Oregon, advised by Prof. [Thien Huu Nguyen](https://ix.cs.uoregon.edu/~thien/), and have spent two summers as an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon AWS (2025, 2026). Before UO, I was an NLP Research Resident at [VinAI Research](https://www.vinai.io/); I received my B.S. in Information Systems from [Hanoi University of Science and Technology](https://hust.edu.vn/).

My research is on **task-aware information modeling**: enabling AI systems to _identify_ the information a task depends on, _represent_ it so it stays reliable when language, genre, or topic shifts, and _reason_ over it across many steps, deciding what evidence to keep, update, or discard. When any of these steps fails, a system can answer confidently from the wrong information, a main reason AI is still hard to trust in high-stakes settings. I want to build retrieval and reasoning systems that learn which evidence to find, keep, and act on.
