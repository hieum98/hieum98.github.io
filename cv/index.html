Professional Summary

NLP researcher and Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Oregon (expected 2027), two-time Amazon Applied Scientist intern, with papers at venues including ACL, SIGIR, EMNLP, and AAAI. My research is on task-aware information modeling: enabling AI systems to identify the information a task depends on, represent it so it stays reliable when language, genre, or topic shifts, and reason over it across many steps. When any step fails, a system can answer confidently from the wrong information, a main reason AI is still hard to trust in high-stakes settings. I want to build retrieval and reasoning systems that learn which evidence to find, keep, and act on.

On FORK-World (677 adaptive-attack scenarios), it cut attack success from 78.4% to 14.1% on average (best baseline: 55.0%) while keeping utility above 85%; on MT-AgentRisk, with environments and tasks unseen in training, from 70.6% to 9.1%.
